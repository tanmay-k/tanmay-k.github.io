            <p class="text-secondary mb-0">Its automated meeting notetaker joins scheduled meetings, records the conversation, generates a transcript, and uses an AI model to produce a concise summary.               <h3 class="h5">Notetaker joins</h3>

            </div>
            <div class="workflow-step">
              <span class="step-number">3</span>
              <h3 class="h5">Recording and AI processing</h3>

              <li>Designed the Admin, Reseller, and Tenant hierarchy and implemented API-key authentication for API-only reseller and tenant access.</li>
              <li>Designed and implemented approximately 20–22 .NET 8 REST APIs for scheduling, retrieving, and managing notetaker sessions through lifecycle states.</li>
              <li>Implemented and deployed an Azure Function integrated with Service Bus for part of the asynchronous meeting lifecycle.</li>
              <li>Integrated Azure Key Vault and Blob Storage, using signed URLs to provide secure direct access to generated meeting artifacts.</li>
              <li>Evaluated .NET unit-testing libraries and demonstrated that xUnit with Coverlet could meet the client’s minimum 70% coverage target; the approach achieved 84% coverage and was adopted by the frontend team.</li>
              <li>Worked with the bot development team to improve the notetaker’s ability to join the correct meeting, increasing the success rate to more than 90%.</li>
