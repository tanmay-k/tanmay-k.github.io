Project case study

Third-Party Agent Onboarding Platform

A digital onboarding experience that helps a financial services provider bring new third-party agents into its network with greater speed, visibility, and consistency.

IndustryFinancial services
Project typeEnterprise web platform
FocusWorkflow automation
My roleSoftware engineer

01 / Overview

Making a complex process easier to complete

The client provides money transfer, check cashing, and bill payment services through its own branches and a network of independent agents.

Before this platform, onboarding an agent required several disconnected manual steps: collecting business and owner information, gathering signed documents, initiating background checks, and coordinating multiple reviews. The project brought those steps into one guided workflow, giving applicants and internal teams a clearer path from application to approval.

Project goals

  • Reduce manual coordination and avoid duplicate data entry.
  • Make application status visible to the right teams.
  • Support consistent compliance and review practices.
  • Give agents a more straightforward onboarding experience.

02 / The challenge

Replacing handoffs with a connected workflow

Many stakeholders

Applicants, operations teams, compliance reviewers, and decision-makers all needed the right information at different points in the process.

Sensitive information

Business, ownership, identity, and background-check information had to be collected carefully and handled with appropriate controls.

Variable review paths

Applications could require different checks and approvals, so the workflow needed to be structured without becoming rigid.

03 / Solution

A guided journey from application to decision

The platform organizes onboarding into clear stages. Each stage captures the information needed for the next decision and keeps progress available to the teams responsible for moving the application forward.

1

Application and profile creation

The agent submits business details, ownership information, and supporting documents through a structured experience.

2

Validation and due diligence

Required information is checked and background or compliance reviews are initiated as part of the application flow.

3

Review and approvals

Internal teams can review a complete application, request follow-up information, and record decisions in a consistent way.

4

Onboarding completion

Approved agents move forward with a clear status and a reliable record of the completed onboarding journey.

04 / Technologies and practices

The building blocks behind the platform

Web application

Responsive forms and role-based views for applicants and internal users.

REST APIs

Service integrations for validation, workflow actions, and status updates.

Data persistence

Structured storage for applications, documents, reviews, and audit history.

06 / Outcomes

A stronger foundation for agent growth

The platform replaced a fragmented, manual process with a more transparent and repeatable onboarding journey.

07 / Reflection

What I learned
