Project case study

Digital Lending & Invoice Financing Platform

A financial-services platform that helps small and medium-sized businesses access and manage short-term financing through loans and eligible invoice-based funding.

IndustryFinTech and lending
Project typeLoan management platform
Core stackJava, Spring Boot, Maven
My roleSoftware engineer

01 / Overview

Managing the complete financing lifecycle

The platform supports businesses from their first financing application through credit assessment, approval, disbursement, repayment, and loan closure.

It also supports invoice financing, allowing eligible business invoices to be captured, validated, and used as the basis for obtaining short-term funding. Internal teams can review applications, monitor active loans, manage exceptions, and keep customers informed throughout the process.

Project goals

  • Provide a consistent digital lending journey.
  • Support invoice-based financing workflows.
  • Improve visibility into loan and repayment status.
  • Strengthen operational traceability and reliability.

02 / Key capabilities

One platform for lending operations

Customer and application management

Business registration, customer information, financing requests, terms, and application status are managed in a structured workflow.

Credit and invoice assessment

Customer and business information supports eligibility decisions, while invoice data is validated before financing amounts are calculated.

Loan servicing and collections

Approved loans, disbursements, installments, outstanding balances, overdue payments, and collection actions can be tracked over time.

03 / Financing journey

From customer request to repayment

The application connects customer-facing activity with internal lending operations. Each stage gives the next team the context needed to make decisions and keep the financing record accurate.

1

Onboarding and application

A business is registered and submits a loan or invoice financing request with the required information, amount, and terms.

2

Assessment and approval

Customer, business, and invoice information is reviewed to determine eligibility, financing limits, and approval decisions.

3

Disbursement and servicing

Approved financing is disbursed and tracked through repayment schedules, installments, balances, and status changes.

4

Collections and closure

Payments and overdue amounts are recorded, collection workflows are supported, and completed financing is closed with a durable history.

05 / My role

Improving the platform from codebase to runtime

I worked on the development, maintenance, modernization, and operational reliability of application components supporting the lending lifecycle.

  • Consolidated 11 fragmented repositories into 5 to simplify the distributed monolith.
  • Unblocked the critical migration from Java 8 to Java 21 by reducing dependency complexity.
  • Optimized batch processing and reduced execution time by 40%.
  • Implemented asynchronous logging to reduce the impact of logging on application work.
  • Implemented audit logs for critical operations and improved traceability.
