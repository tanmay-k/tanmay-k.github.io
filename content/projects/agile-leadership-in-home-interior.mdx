            <div className="workflow-step">
              <span className="step-number">2</span>
              <h3 className="h5">Select and align specialists</h3>
              <p className="mb-0">I evaluated contractors for specific work, explained the delivery model, and aligned each person on their responsibilities and hand-offs.</p>
            </div>
            <div className="workflow-step">
              <span className="step-number">3</span>
              <h3 className="h5">Sequence dependent work</h3>
              <p className="mb-0">I planned the order of activities so one contractor’s output enabled the next contractor to start, reducing clashes and idle time.</p>
